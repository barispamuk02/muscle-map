# Muscle Map: first-users launch, 17 October 2026

## Product and positioning
- Destination: https://muscle-map.app/
- Audience: Russian-speaking people who train at home or at a gym.
- Primary job to be done: pick a muscle, find exercises, make a workout and save results.
- Free-first positioning: useful muscle atlas, exercise library, workouts and diary; Premium progression is optional.
- Premium listed price: $3.99/month. Always check actual checkout price, renewal terms and payment page before public promotion.
- No statements about proven medical outcomes, customer counts, rankings or testimonials.

## Before distributing links
1. Open the homepage as a guest on mobile and desktop; confirm muscles, exercises, workout planning and navigation appear. This is an acceptance check, not a request to create another paid account.
2. Open account/password-reset flow without requesting another OTP; confirm the UI is reachable.
3. Open one public exercise URL from sitemap, check canonical and human-readable content. Do this for a random sample across muscle and exercise pages.
4. Review published terms, privacy, refunds, auto-renewal disclosure, support contact and applicable legal requirements before inviting paid signups. Do not assume these are complete.
5. Verify robots.txt, sitemap.xml and sitemap-seo.xml in Search Console; request indexing of representative pages. Review duplicate sitemap URLs separately.
6. Confirm payment and alert monitoring remain stable; do not run new live charges.

## Week 1: first users (no ad spend)
Day 1: publish one short demonstration of muscle selection -> workout -> diary, with a link to the site. Explain that this is a new product and ask for feedback.
Day 2: post a 30-45 second screen recording on YouTube Shorts demonstrating selecting exercises for chest/back. Include a site link and accurate disclosure.
Day 3: post a practical text guide in a relevant Telegram community only if that community allows promotion; ask members what is missing.
Day 4: make an English-language demonstration or landing post using existing bilingual UI; do not claim full English coverage without checking.
Day 5: contact five relevant microcreators/coaches for feedback, not fabricated endorsements.
Day 6: examine actual mobile UX on Samsung/Android and fix the top reported friction point.
Day 7: review anonymized analytics and honest user replies. Decide whether to expand traffic.

## Measurement
- Visitors from tagged campaign links (utm_source / utm_medium / utm_campaign).
- First meaningful engagement: muscle selected, exercise opened, workout started or saved.
- Return visit within 7 days.
- Account registration and verified email conversion.
- Checkout intent -> paid conversion only where tracking is consent-compliant and trustworthy.
- Mail delivery and billing risk alert health.
Avoid declaring acquisition or revenue metrics before measurement.

## Release gates and risks
- Do not buy social ads before basic analytics and consent/privacy review.
- Never publish production API keys, webhook signatures, real user identifiers or billing event data.
- Do not assert that GitHub Actions schedule is guaranteed to execute on time.
- No artificial paid orders or chargebacks for marketing validation.
- Current scope excludes changing billing, legacy subscriptions, database contents and the protected Neon backup branch.
